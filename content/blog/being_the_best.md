I tend to drift  
towards what I'm good at

Success gets me drunk in dopamine

Always wanting more  
it's safety

<br/>

So, I shape my life  
iterating on things I succeed

it's becoming a prison

I'd like to do the things I suck at  
And I'm already too good at forcing myself
